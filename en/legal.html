1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences (such as language, font size, etc.) for a period of time.

2. Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for basic site functionality.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your browsing preferences.

You can manage or delete cookies at any time from your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view stored cookies, delete individual or all cookies, and block cookies from specific websites or all sites.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page — 3–5 business days. A presentation website (3–5 pages) — 5–7 days. We define exact timelines in the first consultation after discussing scope.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Just an idea and a way to contact you. We help you prepare texts, photos, and logo. If you already have materials, great! If not, we help with structure and stock photos.

How does payment work?

50% deposit before starting, 50% upon completion and approval. We accept bank transfers. All terms are outlined in the contract.

Will the website work on phones?

Yes. All websites we build are fully responsive and look great on mobile, tablets, laptops, and desktops. Mobile-first is our standard.

What does "round of revisions" mean?

We fix minor errors, change texts, update contact details, and edit some elements for free. It doesn't include creating new pages or major design changes.

What if I don't like the design?

Your satisfaction is our priority. In the design phase, we work together on the style before coding. You see the mockup and approve it. If something isn't right, we adjust until it's perfect.

Do I need my own domain and hosting?

We help you choose and register a domain and hosting. We recommend reliable and affordable providers. All technical setup (DNS, SSL, deployment) is handled by us — you don't need to worry about it.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes, if the site is on WordPress. For HTML/CSS sites, we provide a brief guide for simple changes. We can also set up a simple admin panel to edit content without coding.
